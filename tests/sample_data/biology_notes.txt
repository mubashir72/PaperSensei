Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. Chlorophyll absorbs light in chloroplasts. Glucose stores chemical energy and can be used in cellular respiration.

Cellular respiration releases energy from glucose. Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and produces carbon dioxide and water. Much of aerobic respiration takes place in mitochondria.

Plants carry out both photosynthesis and respiration. Photosynthesis depends on light, while respiration occurs during day and night. Stomata are small pores in leaves that allow gas exchange.
